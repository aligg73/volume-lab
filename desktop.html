Volume lab

AR prototype · tap to fold
  1. Tap the golden thousand cube. 1000 cm³ becomes exactly 1 litre of water.
  2. Pick up the jug and tilt it to pour into the jar, bowl and flask. Watch the total: it never changes.
  3. Peel a red sticker (press and drag it off). Water jets out, faster from the deeper holes.

Drag the jug to carry it · mouse wheel, arrow keys or the slider to tilt · drag empty space to look around · double-click the jug to set it down.
